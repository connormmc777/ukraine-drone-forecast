The reason the debate exists

How each model's MAPE grows with the forecast horizon

At a 1-week horizon, the rolling forecast wins on measured MAPE. Past 2–3 weeks they converge. Past 1 month, CAGR pulls ahead — it has more memory to work with. Hover to see either model's expected MAPE at any horizon.

The lines cross around week 2. To the left, rolling wins the sprint. To the right, CAGR wins the marathon. Neither is right in general. Different questions need different math.
